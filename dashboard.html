Watches live streams 24/7, catches the best moments, and turns them into vertical Shorts — before the streamer uploads their own video. Runs on your PC, in this browser tab.

1 · StartAdd streams below (search YouTube live, or paste a link) → language is auto-detected (Roman Urdu/Hindi captions by default) → pick styles on the Styles page → star your favorites in order → press Start.
2 · Middle (automatic)The watch loop tracks speech + live chat + loudness, finds the best moment, and renders a vertical Short with the next favorite style (clip 1 → favorite 1, clip 2 → favorite 2, cycling). No key needed; a free Gemini key (Settings) makes the picking smarter.
3 · EndClips land in the Gallery below — watch them and press Download, or let the auto-post queue post them to YouTube. Your style selection reaches the tool only through this Styles page — no chat round-trip per clip.
